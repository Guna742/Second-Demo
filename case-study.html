Case Study

NeoBank Prime

Redefining digital banking for the Gen Z savvy investor.

📖 8 min read
Client: NeoBank Role: Product Design · Development Year: 2024 Duration: 10 weeks

The Challenge

Traditional banking apps are cluttered, slow, and uninspiring. NeoBank had a revolutionary financial product but a legacy interface that leaked users at every touchpoint. They needed a mobile-first experience that felt less like a spreadsheet and more like a lifestyle companion — something that would resonate with users under 30.

The Strategy

We adopted a "Dark Mode First" approach to align with the user's daily habits. We simplified navigation into three core actions: Pay, Save, and Invest. By utilizing haptic feedback and micro-interactions, we made every transaction feel tangible and rewarding.

Design Process

Over 3 weeks, we conducted 24 user interviews, built 12 prototypes, and ran 8 rounds of usability testing. We iterated on the card component 47 times until the interaction model felt invisible — like the app was reading the user's mind.
